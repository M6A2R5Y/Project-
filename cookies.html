Cookie Policy and Third-Party SDK Audit

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026 | Zero Third-Party Tracking Pixels

1. Overview of Our Storage Principles

Bliss Autos Kenya Ltd ("Bliss Autos") operates under a strict data-minimization and privacy-by-design architecture. Our website (https://www.blissautos.co.ke) uses only strictly necessary first-party browser `localStorage` entries required to operate client authentication and remember your cookie banner acknowledgement.

We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site behavioural profiling pixels, session replay scripts, or device fingerprinting tools.

2. Complete Inventory of First-Party Storage Keys

Below is the exhaustive list of first-party browser storage keys used by this platform:

Storage Key Type Category Purpose Lifespan / Expiry
bliss_cookie_consent First-Party LocalStorage Strictly Essential Records that you have reviewed and dismissed the cookie notice banner so it does not obstruct navigation on every page load. Until cleared by user
authToken First-Party LocalStorage Strictly Essential Stores your signed JSON Web Token (JWT) when you explicitly sign in to the Client Portal. 8 hours (server-enforced JWT expiry)

chat_customer_name / chat_customer_email First-Party LocalStorage Functional (Opt-In) Set only if you explicitly submit the Support Desk intake form and tick the consent box. Until cleared via browser or button below

3. Third-Party SDK and External Request Audit

We perform automated deterministic audits across all HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files to verify that zero unauthorized third-party SDKs are loaded:

  • Analytics & Telemetry SDKs: None (0). No Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Mixpanel, Segment, or Hotjar scripts are included.
  • Advertising & Retargeting Networks: None (0). No Meta/Facebook Pixel, DoubleClick, TikTok Pixel, or LinkedIn Insight tags are present.
  • External Font Trackers: None (0). We use a native operating-system font stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif) so your browser never transmits your IP address to external font CDNs.
  • Embedded Media: Vehicle photographs and SVG icons are hosted 100% locally on our first-party origin without external iframe embeds that set third-party cookies on page load.
