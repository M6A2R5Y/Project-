Submit a Personal Data Deletion Request

Under Section 40 of the Data Protection Act of Kenya (2019) and GDPR Article 17, you may request permanent deletion of your customer account, inquiry logs, and support desk records. Requests are executed immediately for active web records and confirmed within 72 hours.

Email Subscription & Unsubscribe Portal

Every email sent by Bliss Autos contains a direct one-click unsubscribe link (and RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe header). You can also unsubscribe or manage your email preferences directly below without logging in.

Statutory Retention Exceptions

Where you have completed a physical motor vehicle purchase or NTSA TIMS logbook transfer, statutory tax invoices and transfer records are retained in an offline compliance archive for seven (7) years as required by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) and the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act (POCAMLA). All web accounts, marketing preferences, and chat inquiries are erased immediately upon request.
