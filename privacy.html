Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Bliss Autos Kenya Ltd ("Bliss Autos", "we", "our", or "us") operates the website https://www.blissautos.co.ke. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information you share when viewing inventory, inquiring about motor vehicles, submitting credit applications, or scheduling showroom inspections.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal data is collected, used, stored, and disclosed in compliance with the Data Protection Act of Kenya and international data protection standards.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that enables us to process vehicle sales, test drive reservations, financing inquiries, and statutory registration requirements:

  • Contact Identification Data: Full legal name, national identity number or passport number, personal telephone number, physical address, and email address.
  • Vehicle Transaction Data: Inquiries regarding specific car makes, models, VIN numbers, preferred specifications, trade-in vehicle details, and test drive logs.
  • Financing and Asset Verification: Employment status, monthly income estimates, banking institution preferences, and financing term selections when you submit a pre-qualification request.
  • Technical and Usage Data: Internet protocol (IP) address, browser classification, operating system, page response latency, and navigation pathways across our inventory catalogue.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Your personal information is processed strictly for legitimate dealership and legal purposes:

  • Facilitating vehicle inspections, reservations, sales agreements, and logistics.
  • Processing statutory ownership transfers with the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA TIMS portal).
  • Submitting customer-approved asset finance requests to licensed banking partners.
  • Responding directly to customer inquiries, test drive requests, and customer service requests.
  • Complying with statutory accounting, anti-money laundering (AML), and tax documentation requirements under Kenyan law.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information may only be disclosed under the following controlled circumstances:

  • Financial Institutions: With your explicit consent, we provide relevant financial details to partner commercial banks or asset lenders processing your vehicle loan.
  • Government and Regulatory Bodies: Statutory submissions to NTSA, Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), or law enforcement agencies when legally required by court order or statute.
  • Professional Service Providers: Certified motor vehicle inspectors, tracking system installation partners, and licensed clearing agents handling imported consignments on your behalf.

5. Data Retention and Security

We implement industry-standard technical and operational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure. All digital transactions and transmissions are encrypted via SSL/TLS encryption. Physical documentation related to vehicle ownership transfer is stored in secure, restricted-access dealership archives.

We retain transaction records for a period of seven (7) years following completion of a vehicle sale to satisfy statutory accounting and motor vehicle licensing regulations.

6. Your Rights Under Data Protection Laws

Subject to applicable legal constraints, you maintain the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to be informed about how your data is being gathered and used.
  • The right to access a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request rectification of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated data.
  • The right to object to or restrict processing of your information where applicable.
  • The right to request erasure of your data when retention is no longer required by statutory law.

7. Cookie and Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes essential technical cookies to remember session authentication, search filters, and inventory display preferences. We do not deploy third-party behavioural ad tracking networks or intrusive user-monitoring pixels.
