Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026 | Data Protection Act (Kenya) & GDPR Compliant

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, minimize, safeguard, and erase personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act of Kenya (2019) and international privacy standards.

2. Data Minimization ("No Unnecessary Data" Policy)

We strictly enforce the principle of data minimization. On our digital platform, we collect only the minimum personal information necessary to respond to your request:

  • Online Vehicle Inquiries & Support Desk: Only your Name, Email Address, and Inquiry Message. We never request your National ID number, Passport number, KRA PIN, Date of Birth, residential address, or banking credentials on web inquiry forms.
  • Client Portal Registration: Only your Name, Email Address, and a salted-and-hashed password (hashed using `bcrypt` with 10 salt rounds).
  • In-Person Statutory Transfer (Offline Only): Statutory identification required by the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA TIMS) is collected strictly at contract execution stage for buyers completing a physical vehicle purchase.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

We process your information only where we have a clear legal basis:

  • Explicit Consent: When you tick our un-checked consent box to submit a vehicle inquiry, start a support consultation, or opt in to email updates.
  • Contractual Necessity: To schedule vehicle inspections, issue pro-forma invoices, or process holding deposits at your request.
  • Legal Obligation: To execute statutory logbook transfers with NTSA TIMS and comply with tax accounting requirements for completed vehicle sales.

4. Age Consent and Protection of Minors (18+ Requirement)

Motor vehicle purchase contracts, test drives, and asset financing agreements in the Republic of Kenya require legal majority. Our website and services are directed exclusively to individuals aged eighteen (18) years and older.

  • Every interactive form on our platform requires explicit confirmation that the user is 18 years of age or older.
  • We do not knowingly collect, store, or process personal data from children or minors under the age of 18 without verifiable parental or legal guardian consent.
  • If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a minor has submitted personal information to Bliss Autos, please notify privacy@blissautos.co.ke or use our Data Deletion Portal, and we will immediately purge the information from our servers.

5. Email Communications & One-Click Unsubscribe Links

  • Strict Opt-In Only: We never add inquiry or registration emails to marketing mailing lists unless you explicitly tick the optional email updates checkbox (which is always un-checked by default).
  • Mandatory Unsubscribe Link in Every Email: Every email communication sent by Bliss Autos includes a visible, one-click Unsubscribe link in the footer as well as standard RFC 8058 List-Unsubscribe headers.
  • Instant Portal Unsubscribe: You can also revoke email consent at any time without logging in at our Data Deletion & Email Unsubscribe Portal.

6. Third-Party SDKs and Data Sharing Audit

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We have audited our codebase to guarantee that zero third-party behavioural tracking SDKs, advertising pixels, or external font trackers are embedded on our site (see our Cookie Policy & SDK Audit).

7. Your Rights & Self-Service Data Deletion Requests

Under the Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, port, restrict, or permanently erase your personal data:

  • Self-Service Erasure: Visit our Data Deletion Portal to immediately delete your client portal account, inquiry submissions, and support desk logs.
  • Direct DPO Request: Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@blissautos.co.ke. All requests are fulfilled within 72 hours.
