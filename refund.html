Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 29, 2026 | Zero Hidden Cancellation Fees

1. Summary of Consumer Protection Commitments

Bliss Autos Kenya Ltd (Registration No. PVT-2024-089412) enforces a transparent, buyer-friendly refund and cancellation framework. We do not charge hidden administrative penalties, restocking fees, or deceptive forfeiture clauses when a prospective buyer cancels a vehicle reservation prior to final ownership transfer.

2. Vehicle Holding Deposits (100% Refundable)

  • 7-Day Reservation Window: A prospective buyer may place a refundable holding deposit to reserve a specific vehicle unit for up to seven (7) calendar days while arranging an in-person mechanical inspection or bank asset finance evaluation.
  • Unconditional Cancellation Prior to Contract Execution: If you decide not to proceed with the vehicle purchase for any reason before signing the final Vehicle Sale Agreement and initiating the NTSA TIMS transfer, 100% of your holding deposit is refunded without deduction.
  • Independent Mechanical Inspection Contingency: If an independent mechanical inspection reveals any undisclosed structural, engine, or transmission defect, you are entitled to an immediate 100% refund of all deposited funds.

3. Asset Financing Declines

Where a vehicle reservation is contingent upon commercial bank asset financing and the lending institution declines the credit application or offers terms that the buyer does not accept, Bliss Autos will refund 100% of the buyer's holding deposit upon presentation of the bank's decision notice.

4. Post-Delivery Inspection & Statutory Warranty Returns

  • Every vehicle is delivered with a signed 150-Point Technical Inspection Certificate and verified odometer record.
  • If a material mechanical defect in the engine block, gearbox, or chassis frame emerges within fourteen (14) calendar days or 500 kilometers of delivery (whichever occurs first) that was not disclosed on the pre-delivery inspection report, the buyer may elect either:
    • Full mechanical rectification at Bliss Autos' sole expense at an accredited service center; or
    • Return of the vehicle in its delivered condition for a full refund of the purchase price, including reimbursement of the Ksh 3,500 NTSA TIMS transfer fee.

5. Refund Processing Timelines & Payment Method

  • All approved refunds are processed within three (3) business days of receiving a written cancellation or refund notice.
  • To comply with Kenyan anti-money laundering regulations (POCAMLA), refunds are remitted strictly via electronic bank transfer (RTGS / EFT) back to the originating bank account from which the funds were received.
  • Zero processing fees or bank handling surcharges are deducted by Bliss Autos.

6. How to Submit a Refund or Cancellation Request

You may initiate a reservation cancellation or refund request at any time without telephone hold queues by contacting our finance office in writing or visiting our showroom:
