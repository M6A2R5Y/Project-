3. Vehicle Reservations and Holding Deposits

  • A prospective buyer may place a holding deposit to reserve a vehicle for a period of up to seven (7) calendar days pending final financing approval or mechanical inspection.
  • Holding deposits are fully credited toward the final purchase price upon execution of the Vehicle Sale Agreement.
  • Should a prospective buyer decline the purchase following an independent mechanical inspection that reveals undisclosed material structural defects, the holding deposit will be refunded in full within three (3) business days.

4. Technical Inspection and Pre-Delivery Certification

Every pre-owned vehicle offered by Bliss Autos undergoes a certified 150-point technical inspection covering chassis structural integrity, engine diagnostics, drivetrain performance, brake systems, electrical controls, and safety equipment. A comprehensive inspection certificate and verified odometer record are made available to the buyer before contract execution.

5. Financing and Asset Lending

  • Bliss Autos assists customers in coordinating financing options through accredited commercial banking partners and licensed microfinance institutions in Kenya.
  • Financing approvals, interest rate margins, monthly repayment terms, and collateral conditions remain at the sole discretion of the lending institution.
  • Vehicle release is conditioned upon receipt of an irrevocable Letter of Undertaking from the approved bank or confirmed settlement of funds.

6. Ownership Transfer and Vehicle Registration

  • Legal title and ownership transfer are executed via the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) Transport Integrated Management System (TIMS) portal.
  • Bliss Autos initiates the TIMS transfer within forty-eight (48) hours of receiving full payment and required buyer documentation (National ID, KRA PIN certificate, and active NTSA TIMS account profile).
  • The physical logbook is delivered directly to the buyer or to the financing institution holding a charge over the asset.

7. Payment Methods and Anti-Money Laundering Compliance

To adhere to the Proceeds of Crime and Anti-Money Laundering Act of Kenya (POCAMLA), payments must be conducted through traceable electronic bank transfers (RTGS / EFT), direct deposits into our official corporate bank accounts, or verified mobile money merchant tills. Cash payments exceeding statutory thresholds will not be accepted at our showroom counters.
