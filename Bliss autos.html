Our Technical Standards and Guarantees

We remove uncertainty from motor vehicle acquisition through verifiable mechanical and statutory checks.

Chassis & Structural Verification

Each chassis undergoes alignment diagnostics and ultrasonic paint-depth testing to confirm zero collision reconstruction or unibody structural damage.

Engine & Powertrain Diagnostics

Comprehensive computer diagnostic scan for error codes, transmission gear change evaluation, cylinder compression testing, and cooling pressure testing.

Verified Odometer Records

Export certificates, Japanese CarVX/QISJ inspection sheets, and verified service maintenance logs to safeguard against odometer rollback fraud.

Clean Title & Encumbrance Clearance

Thorough verification with the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) and the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) confirming clean legal ownership.
