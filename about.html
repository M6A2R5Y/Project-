About Bliss Autos Kenya Ltd

Verified automotive inventory, transparent vehicle histories, and regulated dealership operations in Nairobi, Kenya.

Corporate Identity & Dealership Background

Bliss Autos Kenya Ltd (Company Registration Number PVT-2024-089412, KRA Tax PIN P051982341K) was established to provide motor vehicle buyers with a transparent, documented, and technically verified vehicle acquisition process. Operating from our showroom and service hub along Mombasa Road (Plot LR No. 209/11452) in Nairobi, we supply inspected local pre-owned vehicles alongside vetted Japanese, European, and American imports.

We eliminate uncertainty from vehicle purchases by replacing vague sales pitches with documented mechanical diagnostics, verified mileage logs, and all-inclusive pricing with zero hidden fees.

Our Core Technical & Consumer Standards

150-Point Technical Inspection

Every vehicle entering our showroom undergoes a documented mechanical evaluation including engine compression tests, OBD-II electronic diagnostic scans, suspension integrity tests, and brake system verification.

Verified Vehicle History

We verify export certificates, QISJ/CarVX inspection sheets, previous registration records, and service logs. We never list vehicles with structural collision history, flood damage, or tampered odometers.

Regulated Ownership Transfers

All title transfers are processed directly through the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA TIMS) portal, providing legal certainty and clean title transfer within 48 hours of vehicle settlement.
