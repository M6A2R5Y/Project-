About Bliss Autos

Verified automotive inventory, transparent vehicle histories, and dependable dealer service in Nairobi, Kenya.

Our Dealership Heritage

Bliss Autos Kenya Ltd was established to provide motor vehicle buyers with a transparent, dependable, and technically sound vehicle acquisition process. Operating from our showroom and service hub along Mombasa Road in Nairobi, we specialize in high-grade local pre-owned vehicles and vetted Japanese, European, and American imports.

We eliminate uncertainty from vehicle purchases by replacing vague sales pitches with rigorous mechanical diagnostics, verified mileage records, and transparent pricing structures.

Our Core Standards

150-Point Technical Inspection

Every vehicle entering our showroom undergoes a comprehensive mechanical evaluation including engine compression tests, electronic diagnostic scans, suspension integrity tests, and brake system verification.

Verified Vehicle History

We verify export certificates, auction sheets, previous registration records, and service logs. We never list vehicles with structural collision history, water damage, or tampered odometers.

Regulated Ownership Transfers

All title transfers are processed directly through the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA TIMS) portal, providing legal certainty and clean title transfer within 48 hours of vehicle settlement.
